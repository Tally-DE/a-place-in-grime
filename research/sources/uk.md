# UK interviews and scene collections

On 23 November 2026 the first UK-wide pass added 18 profiles. It covered London, Glasgow, Manchester, Nottingham and the West Midlands. For the later work in Wales, Northern Ireland, Scotland and more English cities, see [local sources around the UK](local-scenes.md).

| Collection and item checked | What it preserves | Where to pick up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Red Bull Music Academy, Skepta in Barcelona](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/skepta-england-is-grime/) | Skepta names the people in Boy Better Know and describes his move from DJing to MCing. | Find a dated recording that bills DJ Maximum on decks. |
| [Red Bull Music Academy, Kano interview](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/04/kano-interview/) | Kano remembers Flavour radio, D Double E and N.A.S.T.Y. Crew. | Look for a crew recording that names Mak 10 as the DJ. |
| [V&A, The Music is Black](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-music-is-black-large-print-guide) | Search the object labels for Jme’s Mario Paint setup and Shystie’s role in Dubplate Drama. | Trace an early Jme recording to the console from a contemporary credit or his own account. |
| [Astral Black, Lambert & Yoker](https://astralblack.kudosrecords.co.uk/release/ast023/rapture-4d-lambert-yoker) | The label identifies Glasgow producer Rapture 4D and names Polonis and Wiley on the Sub Club set. | Look for the organiser’s original flyer and confirm the date and running order. |
| [Huck, Snowy in Nottingham](https://www.huckmag.com/article/snowy-mc-leading-nottingham-grimes-conquest-uk) | Local MCs describe Nottingham’s youth clubs and the city’s younger generation of performers. | Follow Kyeza into a filmed Nottingham team set and check the MC order. |
| [British GQ, the making of “Pow!”](https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/lethal-bizzle-pow-oral-history-20-years) | Dexplicit and the MCs describe putting the 2004 version together. | Follow Skills to an original Beat Camp credit. Keep the original vocal line-up separate from the 2011 version. |
