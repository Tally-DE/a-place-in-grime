## Derby Archives

The uploader collects audio and mixtapes from Derby across grime, rap and singing. The channel is particularly useful for printed features and production credits. Its stable ID is `UCk6jDJ3Dnt5-gDyf-nv1ZYw`. [Channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCk6jDJ3Dnt5-gDyf-nv1ZYw) · [Uploads](https://www.youtube.com/@DerbyArchives/videos)

**Checked, 23 November 2026.** The channel description names Derby in the East Midlands. The upload list includes Roly, Stanley and Barkz on “'08 Flow” and prints a production credit for Tomek Zyl Music. This pass covered the listing. The title alone cannot date the recording to 2008. [Named upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HMKAiWeQ5yc)

**Pick up here.** Check that upload's credits against an artist or producer copy. Trace one named person back to a set or tape that gives a place and date.

[Channel UCI8h2z481vcwHw_eYzfDylQ](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCI8h2z481vcwHw_eYzfDylQ) · [Creator's production log](https://thftvcollege.blogspot.com/2011/09/)

The uploads list also has a billing naming Eyez, Mystic, Sinseer and Corey, and a THFTVPLAYBACK listing for J Skelly and SMG. The latter two are leads from titles. [Eyez](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_6rWTaGkwps) · [Four-person billing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7mbiHPn5vc) · [J Skelly and SMG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1y_lKZo89k)
