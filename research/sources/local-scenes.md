# Local sources around the UK

These are individual items checked on 24 November 2026. A named guest is a lead. Look for their own account, an introduction or an original credit before placing them in a city.

| Place | Checked | Pick up here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leicester and Northampton | Just Live Records numbers the MCs in its 2016 cypher. Kamakaze opens, Izzie Gibbs takes two turns and Jafro appears seventh. Kamakaze also names the local crew YTS in his interview. [Just Live cypher](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XedFHGAOwgo) · [Kamakaze speaking to TRENCH](https://trenchtrenchtrench.com/features/why-i-rate-kamakaze) | Follow Jafro from the cypher to his own Leicester credits. Find an original copy of YTS’s “Mandem” that credits the MCs. |
| Ipswich | S-StarTV identifies Blaze and Menace separately in its 2011 Brotherhood freestyle. DJ Cameo’s 1Xtra session adds Dusty and D-Bo. The Smokehouse’s homecoming programme connects Brotherhood to the later name Brvtherhood. [S-StarTV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCiviXupgpU) · [1Xtra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gUngb5E0gOc) · [Brighten The Corners](https://www.brightenthecorners.co.uk/events/brvtherhood) | Find an introduction to Dusty or D-Bo that identifies the person on the 1Xtra set. Check where each MC was based. |
| Southampton | Threefold Media interviewed Royal-T in 2009 about “1UP”, the P Money vocal and the DJs who played the beat. In his fabric interview he identifies himself as a Southampton grime DJ and producer. [Threefold Media](https://welcometothefold.wordpress.com/2009/12/07/threefold-media-meets-royal-t/) · [fabric](https://www.fabriclondon.com/posts/introducing-royal-t-its-all-grime-to-me) | Check an original DJ Smallz or Vectra set or tracklist for “1UP”. Keep the location of the DJ separate from the producer’s. |
| Belfast | Plain Sailing’s organisers recall Bloom playing while EMBY and Leo Miyagee took the mic at the Ulster Sports Club. In a separate interview Bloom explains how he found grime in Belfast. [Plain Sailing](https://www.quarantune.world/features/plainsailing) · [Bloom in FACT](https://www.factmag.com/2013/01/07/county-antrim-riddim-fact-meets-rising-grime-producer-bloom/) | Find the event flyer or a dated recording of the Committee Room set. The interview establishes who played, but gives no exact date. |
| Cardiff | Museum Wales holds Local’s oral history under object F2025.41.60. The catalogue identifies his youth-club name MC Hologram. 1Xtra’s Lord of the Mics session separately bills him in 2014. [Museum Wales](https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/31497d65-cefc-31ee-a339-ccc3f921d477/Recordiad-Sain--Audio-Recording-Richard-Sheppard-AKA-Local/) · [1Xtra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpQLzxG9Yxg) | Look for a youth-club flyer or tape that actually bills MC Hologram. Keep the interview date separate from the events he recalls. |
| Newport | Dellux names Critical and Vorn on “NP19” on his own 2014 release. [Dimensions](https://dellux.bandcamp.com/album/dimensions) | Use those spellings to find an individually billed performance for either MC. Establish their city separately. |
| Aberdeen and Paisley | The Black Scotsman interviews Ransom FA about Scottish grime. The interview places him with Gidd Gamz on Risky Roadz and Shogun on “Wake Up”. [The Black Scotsman](https://theblackscotsman.wordpress.com/2016/07/06/meet-ransom-fa-the-grime-mc-whose-bringing-a-sound-of-the-north/) | Find the Risky Roadz upload and check its credits against what Ransom FA remembers. |
| Edinburgh and Glasgow | Nova remembers attending Edinburgh’s Bristo Battles and Spectrum Cyphers and meeting the LVLZ crew after moving to Glasgow. [Nova speaking to DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/longreads/meet-mc-nova) | Find a named recording of a Spectrum Cypher. Check who attended and who actually took the mic. |
| Leeds | MUSIC:LEEDS records Dialect’s battle appearances. The original Don’t Flop film introduces Dialect with his name on screen at 1:06. [MUSIC:LEEDS](https://www.musicleeds.com/supported-artists-2020/2020/12/15/dialect) · [Dialect versus Eyez](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9q-us3JYsY&t=66s) | Trace his Grime-a-Side team through a contemporary line-up and identify the other Leeds performers. |
| Sheffield | Coco names his first crew, Youth Camp, and two early refixes they made. [Coco speaking to TRENCH](https://trenchtrenchtrench.com/features/why-i-rate-coco) | Look for an original Youth Camp upload or sleeve with individual credits. |
| Hull | Untold Hull’s 2016 oral history is interview no. 1156. The credits to Chiedu Oraka’s 2024 mixtape name Deezkid. [Untold Hull](https://untoldhull.org/1156-ChieduOraka) · [Misfit](https://chieduoraka.bandcamp.com/album/misfit) | Find an early Deezkid credit or interview that documents his own work in Hull. |
| Newcastle upon Tyne | H-Man talks to peep about Rap Time under Byker Bridge. Stay Freezin Records lists individual Audio Outlawz credits. [peep](https://www.peep.ltd/journalism/grime-music-artist-h-man/) · [From a Boy to a H-Man](https://stayfreezinrecords.bandcamp.com/album/h-man-from-a-boy-to-a-h-man) | Trace C2-O or Davey Blast from the numbered tracks to their own performance. |

[Sir Hiss speaking to Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/feature/in-session-sir-hiss-dj-mix-bristol-dubstep-140-electro-grime-interview) | Follow one of the label’s other Bristol producers through a named release and their own account. |

## Where the trail stops

- **Newport and Cardiff.** Two Museum Wales catalogue descriptions point to earlier names. In F2025.41.28, Gavin Fernquest recalls Smokeyz TV, MC Natural and Tiny Skitz. In F2025.41.52, Magnus Oboh recalls Brothahood and Stagga. The descriptions were checked. The recordings themselves still need listening to. [Gavin Fernquest](https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/3ef6feed-25ac-37e7-8eff-1a0685853757/Recordiad-Sain--Audio-Recording-Gavin-Fernquest/) · [Magnus Oboh](https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/48d357b4-750a-3e85-bb68-1590b1d3b566/Recordiad-Sain--Audio-Recording-Magnus-Oboh/)
- **Wrexham.** Dave Acton names the Larynx Collective in Museum Wales object F2025.41.45. Find a named member on an original grime recording. Sage Todz’s FOCUS Wales billing records a festival appearance in Wrexham. [Dave Acton](https://museum.wales/collections/online/object/e8ec3ed0-39af-39e9-8d55-fc13ae303fe2/Recordiad-Sain--Audio-Recording-Dave-Acton/) · [Sage Todz interview](https://www.thelineofbestfit.com/features/interviews/sage-todz-finding-power-in-between)
- **Derry.** North West Hip-Hop organiser Shane Deane describes the city’s rap scene. Look for a surviving event programme or recording that actually identifies a grime MC. [Shane Deane interview](https://www.derrynow.com/whats-on/arts-and-entertainment/macd-on-music-best-in-the-north-west-9018839)
- **Swansea.** Sin City’s Window Kid listing is for a visiting Nottingham MC. Check archived bills for a named local opener and follow their own recordings. [Sin City listing](https://www.sincityclub.co.uk/event/window-kid/)
- **Lurgan.** Boxcutter discusses listening to grime in a 2005 interview. His own production credits and town link need checking before listing him here. [Gutterbreakz interview](https://gutterbreakz.blogspot.com/2005/10/interview-with-boxcutter_17.html)
