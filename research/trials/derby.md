## What Wayback recovered

**A line-up and another name.** BritHopTV’s November 2014 post embeds YouTube video `4W_8EJQZqpE`. Opening that video’s Wayback capture from 3 January 2015 revealed Rinse FM’s title naming Eyez, Dubzy, Kaygee, **Directs** and Lox. The page displays a publication date of 19 November 2014. This supports an attribution to the same show. The broadcast date, home cities of every guest and playback of the archived recording are separate questions. The blog, live watch page and archived watch page point to the same Rinse upload. The i-D spelling *Kay’Gee* and Rinse spelling *Kaygee* stay distinct in the notes. [Contemporary BritHopTV embed](https://brithoptv.wordpress.com/2014/11/19/brithoptv-video-set-eyez-dubzy-kaygee-directs-lox-on-sirspyro-grimeshow-rinsefm-grime/) · [Rinse upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4W_8EJQZqpE) · [Inspected January 2015 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20150103140341/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4W_8EJQZqpE&gl=US&hl=en)

**An indexed capture with unusable contents.** For Dubzy’s JDZmedia video, the availability lookup near May 2014 returned a 21 August 2025 capture. Opening it returned HTTP 200, a generic YouTube description and a sign-in requirement. The production credit was legible on the live uploader page. Record that live page as its origin. [Inspected August 2025 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250821223026/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hg9CiK4JD3c) · [Original JDZmedia credit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hg9CiK4JD3c)

**An older crew with an actual Myspace address.** Starting independently of Eyez, Birmingham’s LEGACY project describes **Midlandz Mafia** as spanning Birmingham, Leicester and Derby. In the Continental GT section he names people involved, including Shade One and DJ Total. On Bandmine, the *Site* link on DJ Total’s profile supplies the address `http://myspace.com/midlandzmafia`. This gives a recoverable identifier and a reason to try it. The crew’s three-city connection leaves each member’s own location open. [LEGACY exhibition text, Continental GT section](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/6501bba91dfff5327e8f6304/t/653a52ddd3841a56a44df1ce/1698321118824/LEGACY%2BLARGE%2BPRINT%2B231020.pdf) · [DJ Total profile, Site link](https://www.bandmine.com/djtotal)

I queried that address near June 2007, then opened the returned **5 February 2016** capture. Its title identifies Midlandz Mafia. The exact-path CDX search restricted to 2004–2012 returned zero rows. The evidence supports a surviving 2016 profile page. An earlier roster or recording still needs a period source. An old flyer with a numeric Myspace ID would give the next researcher a new lookup. [Inspected Myspace capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20160205015759/https://myspace.com/midlandzmafia) · [Repeat the 2004–2012 index query](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=myspace.com%2Fmidlandzmafia&from=2004&to=2012&output=json&fl=timestamp%2Coriginal%2Cstatuscode&matchType=exact)

## Handoff

The [current Derby case](../cases/derby.json) carries these open questions across sessions. A later check used a local studio announcement as its starting point and recorded a new artist lead and an unresolved genre question there.

* **@HekkersSnazz.** Seek an independent contemporary producer credit using this exact handle, ideally from the producer or a label. Check whether it identifies a public artist name. Preserve the existing JDZmedia credit whichever way the search goes.
* **Kay’Gee / Kaygee.** Look for a Derby interview, a self-linked account or a label credit explicitly joining the spellings. The 2014 Rinse billing and 2016 interview are already logged.
* **Midlandz Mafia.** Check a period flyer, radio listing or tape inlay for a Derby appearance and credited participants, or an alternate Myspace profile ID. Start from the crew or station and a date range. Keep track of who was billed, who can be heard and who says they came from Derby.

For Derby, the most productive sequence in this trial was **local source → peer interview → original credit**. It reached producers missed by reading a biography alone. The earlier crew supplied a different route into the scene. Both archive experiments with YouTube show why a researcher must open the capture before describing what survived. A local collector and a second reader could still turn the unresolved questions into stronger evidence.
