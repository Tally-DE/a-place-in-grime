## What the trial changed

- Researchers can look up a collection under its old or current name and see exactly which items were checked. [Derby source directory](../sources/derby.md)
- Private work now has local task IDs, a checkpoint example and a validator that accepts files or a folder outside the checkout. Each receipt identifies the packet bytes, even if the file is renamed. [Private runs](../WORKING.md#private-runs)
- The private form accepts multiple files and returns an individual receipt for each successful delivery. Contributors can also paste a single packet.
- The video method records a title card's time and how it was inspected. A visual identification can support a claim even when the audio hasn't been heard. [Source method](../METHOD.md)
